General Information

Microwave Biomedical Research Meets Chicago

The 2027 IEEE MTT-S International Microwave Biomedical Conference is planned for Chicago, a global city known for architecture, engineering, medical innovation, lakefront energy, and a deep academic community.

IMBioC is an international forum for the exchange of ideas and information on state-of-the-art research in microwave and RF theory and techniques that bridge the science and engineering gap as applied to biological systems.

UIC Student Center East Conference venue on Chicago's Near West Side.

Scope

Topics of Interest

Contributions are expected across microwave engineering, electromagnetics, devices, imaging, sensing, and translational biomedical applications.

RF, microwave, and THz circuits and systems for biomedical applications
Antennas and propagation for biomedical applications
Bio-electromagnetics and interaction with biological materials
Electromagnetic imaging and magnetic resonance imaging
Radar and radio sensor applications for healthcare
Wireless power transfer and wireless communication technologies
Wearable and bio-implantable antennas and wireless devices
Pathological, physiological, and biochemical studies with EM waves
Electromagnetic safety studies and regulatory compliance
EM compatibility and interference for biomedical applications
Internet of Things for biomedical and clinical environments
Body sensor networks and body-centric communications
